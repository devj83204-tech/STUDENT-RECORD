❓ How to Use This App

📌 How do I create a class?
On the Home screen, use the "Add Class" field to type a class name (e.g. Class 8) and tap Add. It will appear as a tile you can open.
📌 What is the difference between "Add Subject Only" and "Add Subject with Chapters"?
Use "Add Subject Only" for subjects that don't need further division (like Science till Class 8) — it takes you straight to the attendance sheet. Use "Add Subject with Chapters" when a subject needs to be split further (like Physics, Chemistry, Biology inside Science).
📌 How do I add students to a subject/chapter?
Open the subject or chapter, tap "➕ Add Name" to expand the box, then either register one student with name + parent WhatsApp number, or use "📁 Import Sheet" to bulk-upload from a CSV/Excel file.
📌 How do I mark attendance?
Inside a subject/chapter's student list, simply tap a student's name/checkbox to mark them present for today. Tap again to unmark.
📌 What does the "⚙️ Features" button do?
It expands extra tools: search/filter students, show only unchecked students, voice-based attendance verification, download a Tabular PDF, WhatsApp broadcast, historic calendar logs, and the leaderboard/ranking view.
📌 How do I send a report to parents on WhatsApp?
Inside "⚙️ Features", tap "💬 Broadcast" for a single subject/chapter report, or use the "📢 Bulk WhatsApp Broadcast" page to send every student's own full report to their parent, one chat at a time.
📌 Is my data safe if I close the app or lose internet?
Your data is saved to the cloud automatically as you work, tied to your login account, so it stays safe and syncs back whenever you reopen the app with the same login.
📌 How do I search a specific student's full record?
On the Home Dashboard, use the "🔍 Analytics Profile Tracker" — pick the class, type the student's full name, and tap "Fetch Analytical Logs" to see their subject-wise attendance, download a PDF, or WhatsApp it to the parent.
